# 🚖 Ola Ride Data Analytics | End-to-End Data Analytics Project

**Turning Ride Booking Data into Meaningful Business Insights**

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing Ola ride-booking data to uncover insights into booking performance, revenue trends, cancellation patterns, customer behavior, and operational efficiency using **SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI**.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores ride-booking data to understand business performance, identify factors contributing to ride cancellations, evaluate payment preferences, and analyze customer and driver behavior.

By combining SQL-based analysis, Excel data exploration, and interactive Power BI visualizations, the project transforms raw booking data into actionable insights that can support better business decisions.

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Booking Performance Analysis
- Analyzed total, successful, and cancelled bookings.
- Examined ride volume trends over time.
- Evaluated the distribution of booking statuses.

### 2. Revenue & Payment Analysis
- Compared booking value across Cash, UPI, Credit Card, and Debit Card.
- Identified the leading payment methods by booking value.
- Analyzed revenue contribution by vehicle category.

### 3. Customer Analysis
- Identified top customers based on booking value.
- Examined customer cancellation patterns and reasons.
- Evaluated customer ratings across vehicle types.

### 4. Driver & Operational Analysis
- Investigated driver cancellation patterns.
- Examined reasons affecting ride completion.
- Analyzed driver ratings across vehicle categories.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Booking Performance:** Approximately 62% of bookings were successful, while cancellations represented a significant share of total bookings.
- **Payment Preferences:** Cash contributed the highest booking value, followed by UPI.
- **Cancellation Patterns:** Driver-related issues, including drivers not moving toward pickup locations and requests for customers to cancel, were important cancellation reasons.
- **Revenue Contribution:** Prime Sedan contributed the highest booking value among the vehicle categories shown in the dashboard.
- **Customer Experience:** Customer and driver ratings provide useful indicators for monitoring service quality.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Improve driver accountability and monitor repeated cancellation patterns.
- Optimize driver allocation and pickup management to reduce waiting times.
- Investigate recurring operational issues affecting ride completion.
- Encourage digital payments through suitable offers and incentives.
- Monitor booking success, cancellation reasons, and ratings regularly.
- Use data-driven insights to improve customer experience and operational efficiency.
